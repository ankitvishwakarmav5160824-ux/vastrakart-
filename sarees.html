About VASTRACART

Curated fashion, picked for how you actually dress.

VASTRACART is an online fashion store. We curate women's and men's clothing in one place, choosing styles we think are worth wearing and making them easy to browse and order. We don't make the clothes ourselves — our job is the selection.

The women's collection includes sarees, kurtis and other ethnic and western styles, and the men's collection covers everyday and festive wear. New styles are added to the new arrivals page as they come in.

We're online-only, so shopping stays simple: browse here, order with Cash on Delivery or UPI, or message us on WhatsApp for sizes, availability and product questions. Our aim is to make good fashion easy to find and easy to order.
